# Support — Reimagined for Rhythia 1.3.0

Something not working? Refresh Rhythia first, then check the version shown in the extension popup. Chrome 116+ and compatible Chromium browsers are supported; the separate Firefox package requires desktop Firefox 140+.

If community history is unavailable, open the Data tab in the popup and check **Where history comes from**. If you have not chosen yet, use the small online-history card on Rhythia. You can choose **This device** while the service is unavailable.

If Exodus does not work on a page, switch back to Classic at the top of the popup and send the page address with your report.

- [Report a bug](https://github.com/Rhythia-Reimagined-Extension/Extension/issues/new?template=bug_report.md) — include your browser, extension version, and steps to reproduce it.
- [Suggest a feature](https://github.com/Rhythia-Reimagined-Extension/Extension/issues/new?template=feature_request.md).
- [Email Shuriel](mailto:contact@shuriel.com) for private help, privacy requests, or [security reports](SECURITY.md).

Keep passwords, session tokens, and private login cookies out of reports and screenshots.

For requests about independently collected player history, use the private email and see the [service notice](HISTORY-PRIVACY.md#requests-and-contact). Do not post verification evidence in public issues. Clearing browser history and removing server history are separate actions.

Reimagined for Rhythia is an independent fan project. Official account bans, password resets, and game server issues should go to Rhythia's official support.
