# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-15

Rhythia Reimagined is an unofficial community browser extension for Rhythia.
It is maintained by Shuriel and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Capo
Games.

This policy explains what the extension does with user data. Rhythia and Capo
Games operate their own services and privacy policies.

## Data the extension accesses

The extension accesses information available on Rhythia pages and makes
transient requests to Rhythia APIs to provide profile, score, progression,
comparison, and related features. It also accesses the official Rhythia session
value held in the browser's local storage when needed to make those requests.
The session value is used for the request and is not retained by the extension.

The extension does not read or store browser cookies. Rhythia and Capo Games
operate their own services and determine how their services process data.

## Local data and retention

Extension data is stored locally in the browser. Depending on the features used,
this can include settings, local history, calculated statistics, profile and
score data used by the extension, progression and rank tracking data, comparison
data, and backup configuration.

Local data remains on the device until it is deleted through the extension or
browser, or until the browser's extension data is cleared. It is not uploaded or
synchronized by the extension.

The extension can export local data, import data supplied by the user, and copy
information to the clipboard when the user invokes those actions. Export files,
import files, and clipboard contents are handled on the user's device. Imported
data may be retained as local extension data; clipboard contents may remain
available according to the operating system and clipboard manager in use.

The maintainer does not receive user data through the extension and does not use
maintainer analytics or advertising tracking in the extension. The extension
does not sell or monetize user data.

## Local backups

Backups are optional and disabled by default. When enabled by the user, manual,
automatic, and recovery backups are saved only in a folder chosen by that user.
The extension does not upload, synchronize, or share backup files.

Backups may contain personal information. Users are responsible for choosing a
suitable location, protecting their device and files, and deleting backups they
no longer need. Backup files remain in the selected folder until removed by the
user or by the backup retention settings configured in the extension.

## Permissions and access

The extension requests only the browser access needed to operate on Rhythia
pages, read the Rhythia local-storage session for authenticated API requests,
store its local data, and write optional backups in the folder selected by the
user. Clipboard access is used only for user-initiated copy actions. These
permissions do not give the maintainer access to the user's browser or files.

## Your choices and responsibilities

Users can manage and delete local data and backup files. See
[Data and Backups](DATA-AND-BACKUPS.md) for practical guidance. These actions
do not remove information held by Rhythia or Capo Games.

The extension is an unofficial community tool. Users are responsible for using
it in accordance with the terms that apply to their Rhythia account. Capo Games
may change or discontinue its services, so uninterrupted operation is not
guaranteed.

## Questions and reports

Privacy questions can be sent to `shurieldev@gmail.com`. Security reports should
follow the [security policy](SECURITY.md). Do not include sessions, cookies,
private messages, profile exports, backup files, or other sensitive information
in a public issue or email.

Rhythia and Capo Games control their own server-side processing and privacy
practices. Questions about a Rhythia account or official records should be sent
to the official Rhythia support channels.

## Children's privacy

The extension is not directed to children. The maintainer does not knowingly
collect personal information through the extension.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when the extension's data handling changes. The
latest version is published in this repository.
