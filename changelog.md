## 1.5.0 - Common Ground - 2026-11-07

## Added

- Map types without online history: An option in the popup's Data tab works out map types in this browser from Rhythia's map files: Jumps, Streams, Varied and Quantum, marked approximate. Techs and How this map plays still come with online history.

## Changed

- Online history text: The popup, the online history cards, the Turn on buttons and the policies say plainly what goes to the service: public player and map IDs, Reimagined Search filters and a random number for this copy of Reimagined. The privacy policies are shorter and start with an In short part.

## Improved

- Map types: A type one look already got is shown by the other without asking again.
- Online history limits: When map types or Reimagined Search reach the service's limit, only they wait; player history goes on.
- Load a file: A settings file is saved in one go, so open Rhythia tabs update once instead of once for each setting in the file.

## Fixed

- Titles: A player with exactly 10,000 RP is a Master, as on Rhythia; both looks showed Candidate Grandmaster.
- Reimagined Search: Its note now says Rhythia's Ranked and Qualified lists are checked every hour; it said once a day.
- Popup in a tab: In Firefox, the popup that opens in a tab for Load a file can make the first choice of where history comes from and repair damaged history; both failed there and asked to try again.

# Exodus

## Featured

- Title panel: Redone, in three looks to choose from in the popup, each with a small picture: Road (the Rhythia square, the default), Ring or Track. What the next title or Grandmaster needs is shown large, the way there fills up when the panel first shows, and clicking a title shows when it was reached and how the RP went. Candidate Grandmasters and Grandmasters see the top 30, with what #30, #31 or the place above has.
- Map page as on Rhythia: Reply to a comment, like or dislike a map with the share of likes, and Friends only on the leaderboard when you are signed in.
- Account notices: Excluded, restricted, silenced and inactive accounts show Rhythia's notice (an inactive one with the last score), with Inactive or Excluded in place of the rank; Near your rank and the title give the same reason instead of asking for the top 5,000.

## Added

- Upload your map: The map list has Upload your map, as on Rhythia's own page, when you are signed in.
- Most liked: The map list can be put in order by most liked, as on Rhythia.
- Inactive players: With Include inactive, the leaderboard marks inactive players Inactive.
- Title memory: A profile you saw before opens where it was last time and moves only by what changed since: RP won or lost, and each title won or lost turns over.

## Changed

- Order arrow: In the map list and Reimagined Search, the order's direction is an arrow on the chosen order, and choosing that order again reverses it, so the row fits on one line.
- Comments: Follow Rhythia's limits of 256 characters and 6 lines, with a counter under the box.
- Map types: Come from online history. Techs are told apart better, off-grid maps included, and fewer jumps are taken for spins; saved map types are worked out again. On This device, Exodus reads map files only with Map types without online history turned on; otherwise Playstyle, Scores and Compare show no map types, and the map and score pages offer to turn on online history instead of How this map plays and What the map asks.
- How this map plays: On map pages, and as What the map asks on score pages, it comes from Reimagined's history service. A map it has not read yet is read within a day.

## Improved

- Menus: Scores, Compare and the showcase picker use menus in the Exodus look instead of the browser's own lists. On Scores each choice shows how many plays it leaves, and each Nearby choice has a short note.

## Fixed

- Speed buttons: The preview and replay speed buttons are back under Rhythia's player on map and score pages; an update of Rhythia's site had hidden them.
- Comment replies: Replies are shown under their comment; they showed as separate comments.
- Open tabs after an update: After Reimagined is updated or reloaded, a Rhythia tab that was already open loads the next page in full; before, its pages said online history was off.
- Slow history: A player's online history that answers slowly is shown once it arrives; before, it was given up after a few seconds and the days showed as Not tracked.
- Score page: What the map asks no longer waits for the player's history.
- Auto Restart: Its mod picture shows on map, score and clan pages; it was missing.
- Reimagined Search: After Turn on online history, it no longer says for a moment that the history service did not answer.
- Saved map pages: When Rhythia does not answer and the saved page is shown, its comments and How this map plays still load; they kept loading.
- Saved score pages: When Rhythia does not answer and the saved page is shown, What the map asks still loads, and The day of this play no longer asks to turn on online history when it is on.
- Map comments: When they cannot load, the map page says so instead of No comments yet.
- Own themes: With a picture, text on Reimagined Search's note and, in Lines, on the inventory and the place number on top plays is drawn plain on its own background; it had a soft shadow.

# Classic

## Featured

- Scores page: A player's scores page (Score report) shows Reimagined's Scores list in place of Rhythia's, with an order row (date, accuracy, RP or difficulty; the same order again turns it round), filters for grade, mods, speed and map type in a panel like Rhythia's Advanced Filters, and with online history each play's map type and, in Top, how many of the players ranked near this one it beats, with a Nearby order and filter. Rhythia's chart and Lock, Spin and VR stay; the profile keeps its plain list.
- Statistics in Rhythia's look: With the Tables layout chosen in the popup, Progress and Rank history are lists like Rhythia's: the last 7 days, weeks or months (with the year), what each period changed first with where it ended under it, months that open into weeks and weeks into days, quiet days folded into one row, and Show older. Playstyle is laid out like Rhythia's own sections: four values in plain words, the summary with the mods of the top plays, speed, grades and map types as bars with counts, and the best maps as rows with their art.
- Title progression: A track like Exodus' Track instead of the map: Rhythia's title art along one line that fills with each title's colour, a dot at the player's place, what the next title or Grandmaster needs, and past 10,000 RP the top 30. A profile you saw before moves only by what changed since.

## Added

- Map page: With online history, the map type next to Rhythia's status (Jumps, Streams, Techs, Varied, Quantum), and How this map plays with the share of jumps, streams and techs and how dense the map is.
- Near this rank: With online history, Playstyle says in a few words how the top plays stand against the players ranked just above and below, as Exodus' Near your rank.
- Compare neighbours: With one player added, the tray suggests the two players ranked just above and the two just below.

## Changed

- History periods: In the Tables layout, 30 Days is gone from Progress and Rank history, and the last choice of Last 7 days, Weeks or Months is kept.
- Playstyle values: Full combos instead of an FC ratio and, in the Tables layout, the accuracy change in %.
- Title option: The popup's Progression map is now Progress bar.

## Fixed

- Scores page: Weighted RP, hits and dates are right again; after an update of Rhythia's site every play showed 0 weighted and 0 hits, and some dates were wrong.
- Compare: Map Difficulty and the shared maps show the maps' star rating; they showed Rhythia's difficulty step, around 5.
- Switching from Exodus: A Rhythia page that opened just as Exodus was switched off no longer runs Classic twice.

## 1.4.0 - Within Reach - 2026-10-29

## Added

- Backup: The popup saves the settings and own themes of both looks to a file and loads them back, for another browser or computer.
- Online history: With history on This device, a small card now and then says what online history adds, with Turn on and Not now; from its second time it has Don’t show again. Empty history in both looks, and Near your rank in Exodus, has a Turn on button, and Classic's history from this browser has a small Use online history button under it.

## Changed

- Popup Data tab: Shows what is saved in this browser by kind, each with its size and a way to clear or open it. Recent profile visits are a count with Clear instead of a list, and Reset settings has its own place at the bottom.
- Changelog: Each change is on its own line with a short title, and a release's main features are shown first, under Featured, in both looks, each marked with the look it belongs to.
- Online history text: The popup, the online history card and the Turn on buttons say in one line what is sent, with the privacy policy for the rest.
- Popup: Opens on the tab it was last closed on.

## Fixed

- Popup: With Exodus on, the Experimental label no longer runs into the Classic and Exodus switch.
- Own themes in Firefox: Your own picture, GIF or video shows behind the page in both looks; before, only the built-in pictures did.

## Removed

- Removed Herobrine.

## Notes

- Some of these changes came from your feedback. Thank you!

# Exodus

## Featured

- Near Your Rank: Scores compares your top 100 with the players ranked just above and below you: accuracy, misses, RP, full combos, how often they play, map difficulty and length, speed and map types, the maps they play that are missing from your top, and your maps they rarely play. For players in the top 5,000 with online history on.
- Exodus Expansion: A profile's Maps, Skins and Inventory tabs are now part of Exodus, and a bell next to Settings shows Rhythia's notifications, clan invites included.
- Reimagined Search: The map list has a Reimagined Search tab, Reimagined's own search of Ranked and Qualified maps, not Rhythia's: by map type (jumps, streams, techs or varied), quantum, difficulty, length, mapper and title, and it can leave out the maps in your top 100. It searches Reimagined's copy of Rhythia's Ranked and Qualified lists, read once a day, so results can differ from Rhythia. It needs online history; with it, the map cards in All maps show their type too.

## Added

- Nearby on each play: In Top, each play shows the value of the middle player near your rank on the same map and how many of them you beat. The Nearby filter shows the maps where you beat the players above, are in between, or the players below score higher, and Top can be put in order by most room to improve or best against players near you.
- Neighbours in Compare: Compare suggests the two players ranked just above and the two just below.
- Country leaderboards on maps: A map's leaderboard can show one country, as on Rhythia: Global or any country, with yours first.
- Ranked statuses: The map list can show Rhythia's ranked statuses: Ranked, Qualified, Unranked and Legacy. This replaces the Gives RP switch.
- Recent searches: The search (Ctrl K) lists the last 5 players, maps and clans you opened from it, and while you type, the last 2 that match. They are kept in this browser only, and Clear removes them.
- Scores order and map types: Scores can be put in order by newest, oldest, accuracy, RP or difficulty, and filtered by map type: jumps, streams, techs, varied or quantum.
- Country leaderboard from a profile: The country rank on a profile opens that country's leaderboard.
- New on tabs: A profile tab shows New after an update adds to it, until you open it or for 7 days.
- Supporters: The supporter heart on a profile and on the leaderboard beats, and the ring around a supporter's avatar is thicker.
- Replay speed: A score's replay can be watched at 1×, 0.75× or 0.5× of the speed it was played at.
- Readable picture: The theme editor has a button that sets the picture's blur and darkening (lightening on a white theme) so text reads on it. Both can be changed after.
- Profile Maps: A profile's Maps tab lists the maps the player uploaded, with All maps, Ranked, Qualified and Unranked.
- Profile Skins: A profile's Skins tab lists the player's shared skins, cursors, notes, borders and colorsets, with Download and Copy link.
- Profile Inventory: A profile's Inventory tab shows the player's items and what they wear; on your own profile you can equip or take off a banner, set your name colour's tint, and use a Name Change.
- Ranked countdown: A qualified map shows when it gets ranked, who qualified it and when, and any vetos with their reasons.
- Add to collection: A map page has Add to collection, which lists your collections.
- Remove friend: The friend button on a profile can remove the player from your friends.
- Notifications: A bell next to Settings shows Rhythia's notifications; clan invites can be accepted or declined there, and other notices marked as read.
- Report player: The More button next to Compare has Report player, with Rhythia's reasons and a note, and Copy profile link.
- Collections: Creating, editing and removing a collection happen in Exodus.
- Clans: Inviting a player and leaving a clan happen in Exodus.
- Team Ranked: Players with the Team Ranked badge can qualify a map and veto a qualified one from the map page.

## Changed

- Rank box: Lock, Spin and VR are a column at the left of the rank box, and the box keeps one size in every mode. It lines up with the avatar, and the buttons next to the name line up with it.
- Compare button: Compare is a button next to the player's name, beside the friend button. Both sit on a frosted plate over the banner.
- Activity: Rank shows the global place at the end of each day, week or month, with how far it moved under it.
- Profile switches: Progress, Rankings and Playstyle, and Top, Reigning and Recent, are the same size as the other switches, with brighter text and a clearer chosen tab.
- Scores filters: Grade, mods, speed and map type are in their own row under the search. Filters in use are outlined, and Clear filters resets them.
- Playstyle: The last 14 days and Month in review say how many new top plays were on new maps and how many beat an earlier play of the same map, the biggest improvement, the map types where accuracy rose or fell, and a change of main speed.
- Preview speed: A map's preview plays at 0.25×, 0.5× or the game's speed mods from 0.75× to 1.45×. 1.5×, 1.75× and 2× are gone.
- Own themes: The popup and the theme editor take the colours of the own theme in use.
- Tags: Map types, statuses, difficulties, changelog tags and profile roles look like the buttons, with their colour as a bar on the left edge or in an icon instead of a coloured outline.

## Improved

- Own themes: On a light or busy picture, text that sits straight on it has a soft shadow in the page's colour, links and coloured numbers are easier to read, and names next to a coloured number stay in the text colour.
- Map types: With online history on, Playstyle, Scores and Compare get the types of maps the history service already knows from it, so fewer map files are downloaded.

## Fixed

- Own themes: With two accent colours, the chosen tab of a switch no longer sits on the accent gradient, where its text was hard to read.
- Score replay: Going from a score to a profile stops the replay's music.
- Progress: With less than a day of history, the chart title says today only instead of last 0 days.
- Profiles: After Clear history or choosing This device, a profile opened again no longer fails to draw before it loads.
- Exodus style: With an own theme in use, Lines and Floating in the popup change that theme's style.
- Own themes: With a gradient, picture or video behind the page, a score's replay and a map's preview no longer cover the top bar when you scroll.

## 1.3.1 - Custom Themes - 2026-10-25

## Added

- Pictures, GIFs and videos you add to a theme are kept on this computer and never uploaded.
- Both theme editors list your last 6 uploads, to use again in any theme.

## Changed

- Online history uses an anonymous key for this installation, given out by the Reimagined server. It holds nothing about you or your game account and is removed when you choose This device.
- Choosing This device also removes history already downloaded from the Reimagined server.
- Clear history also clears the list of recent profile visits.
- Saved profile history not updated for 90 days and Exodus' saved pages older than 7 days are now removed once a day, also while the look that saved them is not in use.
- Grade letters in both looks are the ones Rhythia gives a score. Without one, the letter comes from accuracy on Rhythia's scale: SS for 100% without a miss, S from 98%, A from 95%, B from 93%, C from 90%, D from 80%, and F below 80% or for a failed play.
- In the changelog, Reimagined's own notes show one version at a time, with the list of versions beside it and a switch between the Exodus and Classic changes.
- Shorter text in the popup.

## Fixed

- Recent profile visits shows the five newest visits instead of the five oldest.
- When the Reimagined server is slow or does not answer, the changelog shows the notes included with the extension instead of staying on loading.
- When saved history is damaged, the popup no longer locks every setting: only the history settings are locked, and Repair sets the damaged profiles aside and keeps the rest.
- Links in older release notes open the documents.

# Exodus

## Added

- Own themes. In the popup, under Appearance, "New theme" opens a theme editor next to the page, and every change shows on the page at once.
- In the theme editor, start from a ready-made theme, then choose a background (one colour, a gradient, a built-in picture, or your own picture, GIF or video), an accent, contrast and Lines or Floating. The other colours are worked out to stay readable.
- In the theme editor's Advanced tab, click a part of the page, such as the top bar, banners, buttons, panels, lines or text, to change it.
- Profile banners in a theme can show for everyone, only on your own profile, or not at all.
- You can keep up to 24 own themes, listed under the built-in ones in the popup. The pencil on a theme opens it again to change, export or delete it.
- Compare shows who is better at what, with both players' numbers, such as who is stronger on streams and why.
- Compare shows the map types in each player's top 100 (jumps, streams, techs, varied) and how both players do on each type.
- Techs, a map type for short moves where the speed keeps changing.

## Changed

- Map types look at how far and how evenly the cursor moves instead of only the time between notes. Fast jumps, including spins, now count as jumps.
- A map also shows a second type when at least 30% of its notes are of that kind.
- Quantum is shown on top of a map's type, for maps with at least a fifth of their notes in parts placed off the grid, instead of replacing the type.
- Map types are worked out again once, with the new rules.
- On a map page, the note shares add up to 100%, and off-grid notes are shown with the other numbers.
- On a profile, the change shown next to the rank and RP is since yesterday instead of over the last 7 days.
- In Compare, the player picker is a narrower window and lists the suggested players one per row.
- Shorter text on every page, without captions that repeated what the page already shows.
- Profile bios and clan descriptions use the same formatting as the wiki, so clan descriptions also show lists and headings.
- The Data tab no longer lists recent profile visits, which only Classic records.
- In the profile editor, social links follow the same rule as in Settings: they must start with https:// and lead to one of the listed sites, and the help under them says so.
- Info boxes in the wiki are as wide as the other boxes.

## Improved

- Profiles no longer wait for the online history server. The history is filled in when it arrives.

## Fixed

- On someone else's profile, Statistics, Month in review and the status explanations name the player instead of saying "you".
- The Add friend button now lines up with the player's name.
- The Compare window has one scroll bar instead of two, and an older search there no longer replaces the results of a newer one.
- Searching the changelog shows the matching entries.
- On a profile, typing / in a text box or in one of Rhythia's windows no longer jumps to the score search.
- A request that fails no longer replaces a profile's saved history, pinned scores or showcase with empty ones.
- After you sign out or switch accounts, saved pages no longer show the previous account, its friends or its profile tools.
- Clear history and This device take effect in open tabs at once.
- When a profile updates in the background, the score search, filters and page are kept, and a change on your own profile, such as a pin or your bio, is no longer undone by an update that was already on its way.
- A mod filter on profile scores no longer stays active, but hidden, after switching mode or list.
- Title progression shows "Not ranked" instead of negative or zero places for players without a rank.
- In Chrome, opening another page starts at the top.
- On Home, the Spin and VR top 10 no longer stay empty after the page updates in the background.
- On Maps, switching Show explicit goes back to page 1 and stays there.
- On Skins, Copy colors leaves out values that are not colours, and says so when a colorset has none.
- On Support, Gift no longer opens the purchase for yourself when clicked before the page knows who is signed in.
- Closing Settings while it saves no longer skips saving About me or closes Settings opened again, and unsaved changes in Settings are kept when a button opens Rhythia's own Settings, for example for the avatar or banner.
- On phones, the changelog uses the whole width instead of a narrow column, and the top of a collection page is shown in one column.

# Classic

- Own themes for Classic, up to 12, listed under the built-in ones in the popup. Under Appearance, "New theme" opens a theme editor next to the page, and the pencil on a theme opens it again to change, export or delete it.

- Shorter text on Classic's panels.
- Turning off Rank and stat history now also stops recording your history on this device.
- A score card's menu follows the theme's colours.
- On your profile, the settings button looks like the buttons beside it, with a sharper gear icon.
- The pattern in the top bar takes the theme's colours.
- When only the last 3 days of a profile's online history are available, Classic marks it "Online: last 3 days".

- Profiles reuse the profile and pinned scores Rhythia's page has already loaded instead of asking for them again.
- Score cards, the Statistics history and the title progression card are no longer redrawn when nothing in them changed, for example when fresh scores arrive.

## Fixed

- On pages with Rhythia's footer, the whole page is no longer painted as one panel with dimmed text.
- An open profile draws its history again after a setting is changed in the popup or after Clear history, instead of leaving it empty until the page is reloaded. This includes the history table of the Tables layout of Statistics.

- On profiles, Rhythia's own score list and Statistics no longer stay hidden when Score cards or Statistics and playstyle is off, including right after switching it off, or when Classic cannot load the profile.

- With the Tables layout of Statistics, the cards show how much each value has changed, next to the value.

- With Friends list off, the friends counter on your own profile opens Rhythia's friends page instead of Classic's list, also right after switching it off.
- Classic's compare tray no longer stays on screen over pages Exodus shows, for example after switching to Exodus.
- Profile history recorded in two tabs at the same time no longer loses one of the visits.

## Removed

## 1.3.0 - Exodus - 2026-10-20

## Added

- Exodus, a second look for Reimagined (experimental, off by default). Choose the look at the top of the popup.
- A switch to turn off the Friends list.
- A one-time notice in Classic about the Exodus look, with "Try it out!" and "No thanks".

## Changed

- Rhythia Reimagined is now called Reimagined for Rhythia.
- New popup with Features, Appearance and Data tabs. The Classic/Exodus switch is in the header, and each look shows only its own settings.
- Variants such as card styles, Statistics and the Exodus layouts are chosen from small drawings of each one. Parts of a feature are turned on and off with checkboxes under it.
- Recent profile visits are in the Data tab. With Exodus, Data also shows the space its saved pages take, with a separate Clear button that keeps history.
- "Clear Cache" is now called "Clear history".
- The online history choices are now called This device, Both and Community.
- Release notes are downloaded from the Reimagined server instead of GitHub, and only when online history is on. Otherwise the changelog shows the notes included with the extension.
- The changelog lists the changes for each look separately.

# Exodus

## Added

- Own pages for home, profiles, scores, leaderboards (also by country), maps, skins, clans, collections, wiki, changelog and support, built from Rhythia's data.
- Choose in the popup which pages Exodus replaces.
- Lines or Floating style, a list or grid for scores, and a switch for the moving background on Home.
- Pages you've already visited open from a saved copy and update in the background.
- Edit your bio and links, pin and unpin scores, and set your showcase (Supporters) on your own profile. Add friends from their profile and remove maps from your collections.

- In Compare, each shared map goes to the player who got more RP from it.
- Playstyle has a Month in review: plays per day, breaks and how you came back, pace against the month before, new top plays, and which map types you played (streams, jumps, varied or quantum).

## Notes

- Exodus is experimental. If something breaks, switch back to Classic in the popup and let me know.
- Exodus does not send profile visit reports, so that switch is shown only for Classic.

# Classic

- In variant B, the weeks under the chart are listed one month at a time, with older months a click away.

- Scores show 10 per page by default. Choose 10, 20, 50 or All.
- Card style E is the new default. A style you chose before stays.
- The Reimagined changelog looks like Rhythia's own entries: one card with plain lists instead of a box around every line.

## Fixed

- The changelog tabs no longer flicker when the changelog opens.
- The Playstyle note no longer calls a couple of plays "recent play".
- Rhythia's cookie banner no longer covers the page until it's answered.
- "View progression" shows the Reimagined progression map again, not Rhythia's bars.
- The score tabs no longer make the profile wider than the screen on phones.

## 1.2.0 - Going Online - 2026-10-01

## Featured

- Back in business: Adapted profiles, stats, score cards, Friends, themes, and session handling to Rhythia's updated website. Finally...
- Going online: Added a connection to my community history server. Instead of relying only on records collected in your own browser, you can also load available ranking history for the top 1,000 players and additional discovered profiles.
- Reimagined Score Cards: Replaced the Modern/Legacy card choices with three new designs and optional background artwork. Rebuilt the profile score browser with search and numbered pages at the top and bottom. Pick your favorite look, then actually find the score you're looking for.
- Reimagined Playstyle: Replaced the separate Rating and Tempo profiles with a Playstyle view: a player summary, standout plays, and speed and accuracy-grade breakdowns with score counts.
- Reimagined Title Progression: Rebuilt the progression view with a map-like layout and title milestones. More fun to look at, more Reimagined...
- Reimagined Compare: Rebuilt the comparison view with clearer stat differences and a layout for 2 players. The previous version allowed 4.
- Reimagined Popup: Replaced the old popup with a simpler layout and reorganized settings. Again... Hopefully this one gets to stay for a while.

## Added

- Added three history source choices for the new server connection: Cloud Only, Cloud + Local, and Local Only.
- Added an online-history setup card and separate controls for using community history and reporting profile visits. Connections start after your choice; visit reporting requires a separate opt-in.
- Added a connection log in the popup for the new history server: viewed profile, request status, and response time. It shows whether that profile's online history loaded, is missing from the server, or could not be reached.
- Added date-format settings: YYYY-MM-DD, MM-DD-YYYY, and DD-MM-YYYY for score cards and history views.

## Improved

- Expanded the existing Rhythia API integration to load profile details, pinned scores, and more score collections. Added shared caching and request deduplication to avoid fetching the same data repeatedly.
- Reworked profile navigation and loading to reduce flickering and unnecessary rebuilds, and to keep late responses and cached records attached to the correct player.
- Reworked White theme contrast, colors, and readability across cards and pages. White theme needed some love too.
- Polished transitions and glowing accents in Dark and Reimagined themes.
- Improved player and mapper name detection, including profile details fetched for Compare.

## Changed

- Reduced the Compare limit from 4 players to 2.
- Replaced configurable local-history retention and storage limits with fixed limits of 90 days and 25 MB. The previous default storage limit was 300 MB.
- Reorganized profile statistics into dedicated history, ranking, and Playstyle views.

## Fixed

- Updated profile detection and RP/rank extraction for Rhythia's changed page layout.
- Restored profile statistics, score-card enhancements, and Friends controls for the updated website.
- Updated authentication handling for Rhythia's changed login system.

## Removed

- Removed the old backup, restore, and JSON import/export tools. Files you already exported stay where you saved them.
- Removed the protected-profile list and its cleanup exceptions, including protection of your own profile.
- Removed the separate saved Title Progression state used by the old progression view.

## Notes

- Online History & Accuracy: Going online came with a few compromises. The new server history covers ranking and RP; online accuracy history isn't ready for 1.2.0 yet. Your local accuracy history still works. I'm working on the online part, but I didn't want to keep the whole update waiting for it. Yet... is the important word here.
- Why only 2 players in Compare? I think 2 is enough to keep the comparison useful and readable. But I'm open to suggestions if you have a reason to compare more.
- A little Playstyle backstory: The old Rating and Tempo profiles weren't telling you as much as I wanted. I almost removed them... Then it struck me: why don't we make them actually useful? So they grew into Playstyle instead.
- And yes, the popup got remade again: The previous versions were either too complicated, hard to read, or just didn't feel right for the extension. We'll see how long this one lasts...
- Community history depends on the records available on the server. Opting into visit reports helps the server discover additional profiles; it doesn't guarantee history for every visited player.
- Updates keep valid local history and privacy choices. Older local history can be cleaned up under the new limits. Clear Cache asks before removing all local profile history and cached online history.
- Local Only disables all outgoing requests to rhythia.shuriel.com.

## 1.1.0 - UI Polish & Stability - 2026-08-21

## Featured

- Redesigned the Title Progression bar with smoother animations.
- Reworked profile score cards to be more compact, added a scaled accuracy bar, and added an option to keep using the classic layout.
- Cleaned up unused scripts and old styles to make pages load faster.
- Updated the extension icons.

## Added

- Added an option in popup settings to switch between compact score cards and the classic layout.
- Added smooth transitions when switching tabs between Reigning, Top, and Recent scores.
- Added a toggle in popup settings to turn off Easter eggs if you don't want them.
- The in-page Changelog now pulls update notes directly from GitHub so they're always current.

## Improved

- Stat history on profiles now shows the last 7 entries with a "Show more" button so it doesn't stretch the page.
- Switching themes is now instant without page stutter.
- Improved spacing and number formatting in stats history.
- Polished Rating and Tempo Profile cards with better contrast and layout.
- You can now press Escape to close popups and modals.

## Changed

- Clearer highlights on active score collection tabs.
- Advanced Stats now uses the new card layout for Rating and Tempo profiles by default.
- Removed unnecessary browser permissions.

## Fixed

- Fixed broken numbers and calculations caused by commas and thousand separators.
- Fixed green/red change colors (+/-) in stat history looking wrong on certain themes.
- Fixed contrast and readability issues on White and Reimagined themes.
- Fixed daily stat history skipping entries when days roll over at midnight.
- Fixed Title Progression rank not loading on some profiles.
- Fixed score cards breaking after recent Rhythia website updates.
- Fixed score filters (grade/speed) resetting when loading more scores or switching tabs.
- Fixed backup files not restoring saved settings.
- Fixed profile and friends list sometimes failing to load on first visit.

## Removed

- Removed old tools from the `/scores` page since Rhythia no longer links to it from profiles (theme styling is still kept if you visit it directly).
- Removed the old external catalog integration as it was barely used and slowed down navigation.
- Removed the extension badge in the bottom-right corner to keep the screen clean.
- Removed the old changelog timeline bar for a simpler layout.
- Removed unused background scripts and old CSS files.

## Notes

- Behind the scenes, the project was reorganized, cleaned up, and moved to the new GitHub organization.
- Your saved settings and profile history won't be lost with this update.
- The extension source code is open at [GitHub Extension Repository](https://github.com/Rhythia-Reimagined-Extension/Extension).
